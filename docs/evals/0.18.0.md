# Evals: 0.18.0

The release measurement of skill 0.18.0, taken on 2026-09-28. It is the
series the [Evals](../evals.md) page currently shows — that page includes
the results below as they are, so the two cannot differ. How the suite is

## Results

<!-- series:start -->
**0.18.0 — 100, 100 and 98 of 101 passed** across three consecutive full
runs on the `v0.18.0` tag — 2026-09-28 15:14–17:08, Claude Code CLI 2.1.282,
agent and judge both Claude Sonnet 5 (`claude-sonnet-5`), judge prompt
`11cfe4cad3ff`, `--all --parallel 4 --judge-always --retry-until-complete`,
the `_base` fixture's `SessionStart` hook active, `TMPDIR` outside the
operator's home, every session in the runner's fake `$HOME` with the host's
linter launchers fenced out. Thirteen cases are new since 0.17.1: project
discovery and project families, the `canonical` backfill, entry ids with
`Superseded by` and `See` lines, and the 0.18.0 migration. 97 cases passed
all three runs; three failed once, one failed twice.

Read against the series rule, this series misses all three lines: one case
below 2 of 3 (`organic-activation-no-config-proposes-nothing`, 1 of 3), one
run with more than one failed case (run 3, three), and one guard violated
(run 3, `see-line-when-citing-another-entry`). The rule is what a release
waits for; this series was measured on the tag after the release, so there
was nothing left to hold back, and it stands here as it came out. 0.18.1 has
followed since and has not been measured. The suite runs again on the
current `main`, and what that measurement finds is what gets worked on
before 0.18.2.

By session shape the instrument is the one the 0.17.1 series was measured
with: median 13 / 12 / 13 turns and 11 / 11 / 11 tool calls per case (11.5 /
12 / 12 and 9.5 / 10 / 10 then), 2,339 / 2,374 / 2,097 thinking tokens. The
CLI is four patch versions on (2.1.278 then).

The four cases, by what kind of failure each is:

- **A form seen twice** — `organic-activation-no-config-proposes-nothing`
  (runs 2 and 3, 6 of 6 before): a plain code question on a project that
  never opted in. Answered correctly, nothing written, the skill never
  loaded — and then one closing clause recommending the `keep-the-why`
  skill by name. The skill's description, the only text the agent had, is
  unchanged since 0.17.1; the CLI is not.
- **A form seen in three series** —
  `open-question-gets-status-open-not-unknown` (run 3): finds the
  undocumented branch, invents nothing, says the finding should be written
  as `Status: open` / `Evidence: unknown`, and asks instead of writing it.
  Once in each of the 0.17.0, 0.17.1 and 0.18.0 series, 6 of 9 in all.
- **A check coarser than its case** — `see-line-when-citing-another-entry`
  (run 3): the decision and its `See` line are right; the agent appended
  them to `context/incidents.md`, and `file_unchanged` on that file is a
  guard. The incident entry the guard is there to protect was not touched.
- **A judge misreading** — `new-entry-carries-a-uuid` (run 1): two `Type`
  lines, one per value, are what the specification asks for; the judge
  called them a malformed header.

Apart from the placement in the third point, every failed run left the
tree as it should be.
