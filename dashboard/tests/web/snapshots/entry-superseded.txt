      "Source: maintainer interview, 2026-03-01"
  <div class="ktw-body">
    <p>
      "The sync step waits for a full snapshot before it applies any buffered event, even though this adds latency on a cold start."
    <div class="ktw-label ktw-reason">
      <b>
        "Reason"
      <p>
        "applying buffered events before the snapshot landed caused duplicate-then-overwritten state. The ordering is not visible in the code."
    <div class="ktw-label ktw-rejected">
      <b>
        "Rejected alternative"
      <p>
        "replay in parallel and reconcile — the reconciliation was hard to get right, and the ordering made it unnecessary."
    <ul>
      <li>
        "cold start: about two seconds slower"
      <li>
        "warm start: unchanged and no extra memory either"
    <pre>
      <code>
